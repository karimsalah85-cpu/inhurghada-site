## Transfer destination gaps

Do not publish separate Makadi Bay, El Gouna, Soma Bay or Sahl Hasheesh pages until the owner confirms that each is a genuine service with unique, stable facts. Required inputs: one-way/return pricing, exact origin/destination coverage, pickup procedure, vehicle and luggage capacity, child-seat policy, delay/waiting policy, supplements, cancellation, operating hours and original photographs.

## AI-answer content pattern

For each verified question, use a descriptive H2 followed immediately by a 40–80 word direct answer, then limitations and booking context. Prefer compact facts, itinerary, inclusion/exclusion and comparison tables when they clarify a decision. Visible copy and schema must match. FAQ content may help users, but Google generally limits FAQ rich results to authoritative health/government sites; do not promise FAQ rich results.
