## Social content: five ready-to-produce reels

Use vertical 9:16 video, real trip footage, subtitles, and the exact product URL in the profile/link destination. Do not claim guaranteed wildlife, fixed pickup before confirmation, or limited availability unless verified that day.

### 1. Orange Bay: what the day includes

Hook: **What actually happens on an Orange Bay boat trip from Hurghada?**

Shot order: marina departure, reef snorkeling, Orange Bay beach, lunch, return cruise.

On-screen facts: **8 hours · 1 hour on Orange Bay · 2 reef stops · lunch and hotel transfer**

Caption: “Island time or reef time? This trip includes both. Check the current price, inclusions and pickup details before booking direct.”

CTA: **View Orange Bay trip**

### 2. Dolphin House: honest wildlife expectations

Hook: **Will you definitely see dolphins at Dolphin House?**

Shot order: open water, dolphins at a respectful distance when available, reef snorkeling, lunch onboard.

On-screen facts: **Wild dolphins · sightings never guaranteed · 2 reef stops**

Caption: “Dolphin House is a natural habitat, not a dolphin show. The crew follows conditions and wildlife behaviour on the day.”

CTA: **See the full itinerary**

### 3. Full-day diving: who this trip is for

Hook: **Certified diver in Hurghada? Here is what the full day includes.**

Shot order: certification check, dive briefing, entry, reef footage, surface interval and lunch.

On-screen facts: **Certification required · 2 guided dives · equipment +$30 · lunch and transfer included**

Caption: “A proper Red Sea day for certified divers. Dive sites are selected according to weather and sea conditions.”

CTA: **Check diving details**

### 4. Desert safari: sea-to-desert contrast

Hook: **Hurghada is more than the beach.**

Shot order: hotel departure, quad-bike briefing, desert riding, camel ride, Bedouin village and tea.

On-screen facts: **5 hours · quad bike · camel ride · Bedouin visit**

Caption: “Trade the coastline for an afternoon in the Eastern Desert. Exact pickup is confirmed by WhatsApp.”

CTA: **View desert safari**

### 5. Marsa Mubarak: turtles without promises

Hook: **Why do snorkelers visit Marsa Mubarak?**

Shot order: Port Ghalib departure, clear water, reef, turtle or dugong footage only when authentically captured, lunch and return.

On-screen facts: **Natural habitat · wildlife not guaranteed · 2 snorkeling sessions · entrance included**

Caption: “Marsa Mubarak is known for marine life, but nature decides what appears. See operating days, pickup zones and the current package before booking.”

CTA: **View Marsa Mubarak trip**

## Things to do readiness—not immediate ad activation

Schema.org markup improves machine-readable page context but does not enroll inventory in Google Things to do. Participation requires a Things to do product feed, normally through an approved connectivity provider for a small operator. Egypt and Saudi Arabia are not currently listed among the supported Things to do advertising destinations checked for this package, so the immediate work is feed readiness rather than campaign activation:

- Keep stable product IDs and canonical booking URLs.
- Keep price, currency, availability, cancellation wording, images, and landing pages synchronized.
- Preserve direct operator identity on the landing page and Google Business Profile.
- Recheck destination availability and connectivity-provider terms before integration.
