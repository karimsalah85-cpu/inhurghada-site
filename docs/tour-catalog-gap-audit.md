## Condition fields compared and added

Each new product records:

- approximate duration and departure pattern;
- pickup inclusion or the need for a separate pickup quotation;
- core inclusions and exclusions;
- cancellation caveat where tickets, flights, permits, or third-party suppliers can change the standard policy;
- age, health, mobility, swimming, or certification restrictions where relevant;
- weather and marine-condition caveats;
- required documents and suggested items to bring.

## Publication safety

The new products now have positive starting prices and can submit a booking request. The site does not take immediate payment; Daily Red Sea must confirm availability and the final price through WhatsApp before accepting the booking. Record the following operational details before treating a request as confirmed:

1. contracted supplier and emergency contact;
2. adult, child, and infant prices;
3. exact age bands;
4. capacity and operating days;
5. included pickup zones and each remote-zone supplement;
6. cancellation deadline and no-show policy;
7. confirmed inclusions, admissions, fees, and optional extras;
8. supplier-approved images and accessibility/safety restrictions.
